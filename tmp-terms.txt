WHAT ARE WEB3 DOMAINS: Web3 domains reside on a public blockchain and give users ownership of their stored data. One of the key benefits of Web3 domains is simplifying crypto transactions by replacing multiple wallet addresses with a single memorable domain name. Web3 domains also provide identity and access to Web3 applications. Best of all, unlike with traditional DNS domains, when you buy and claim a Web3 domain from Unstoppable Domains, you own it forever - no renewal fees! ARE WEB3 DOMAINS DIFFERENT FROM TRADITIONAL DNS DOMAINS: There are important differences between Web3 domains and traditional DNS domains. Blockchain- based Web3 domains use a decentralized root zone, as opposed to the centralized DNS root zone that is administered by ICANN. Because Web3 domains do not use the same root zone as traditional DNS domains on the internet, Web3 domains do not currently resolve like DNS domains. Web3 domains currently require special browser plugins or network configurations to work with websites in the DNS.
